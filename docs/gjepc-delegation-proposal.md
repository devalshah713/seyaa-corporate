## Before you send

**Fill in:**

1. **Design and SKU counts** — currently 191 designs / 363 SKUs, but check the
   figure in the site footer on the day you send; it moves with the sheet.
2. **GJEPC membership status and number** — the circular states Ordinary Members
   get first preference and Associate Members second. If you are a member, say
   so in the first paragraph, not the fifth.
3. **Your name and title** as the signatory, if it is not Shaival signing.
4. **Company registration name, IEC code** — do not put these in the first
   email; offer them, as the draft does.

**Deliberately left out, and why:**

- **The passport scan.** Do not attach it to this email. It carries the passport
  number, date of birth, place of birth and the machine-readable strip — enough
  for identity theft, sitting in an inbox and in every reply thread. GJEPC will
  collect it through their portal when visa processing begins. Say "follows
  through the portal", as the draft does.
- **Prices.** Not published on the catalogue and not quoted here. The first
  conversation with a buying group is about range and capability; the price
  conversation follows a specific brief.
- **Certifications.** If you hold IGI, GIA, SGL or RJC accreditation, or BIS
  hallmarking, tell me and I will add it — to the email and to the site. For
  Australian and New Zealand buyers this carries real weight, because lab-grown
  disclosure is a consumer-law matter there, not a courtesy. I have not claimed
  anything I cannot verify.

**One thing to check before it reaches a buyer:** the bio says "more than two
decades in the trade". Worth confirming that reads correctly against the date of
birth on the passport GJEPC will hold, since the two documents will sit in the
same file.
